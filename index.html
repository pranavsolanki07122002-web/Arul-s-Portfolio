Hi, I'm Arul.

I spent years reading philosophy at the University of Delhi, mostly people arguing about what is real. Then I picked up a camera and found the argument easier to settle. You look until something looks back.

Now I'm a cinematographer and photographer in Mumbai. I shoot food while it's still warm, rooms while the light is kind, and athletes in the second they decide not to stop.

You look until something looks back.
